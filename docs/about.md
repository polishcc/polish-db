**A little about me**

My name is Robert. 

I’ve been teaching English and Polish for over seven years in Poland, Spain and Japan. I’ve also had a successful career in finance and IT, but languages are the thing I keep coming back to — because I genuinely love them.

I’ve also lived in Denmark, Germany, France, Czechia, Greece, Brazil and, of course, Poland and the UK. And as of 2025, I’ve travelled to exactly 100 countries.

I speak Polish, English and Spanish fluently, I’m highly proficient in French, German and Portuguese, and I can also communicate in Swedish, Russian and Czech. I’ve studied some Chinese and Thai, too.

That mix has taught me a lot about what really makes languages difficult: not just grammar, but sounds, sentence structure, habits of expression and the cultural logic behind the way people communicate.

When we work together, I don’t want to keep you inside a textbook bubble. We use real material: videos, interviews, social media, articles, everyday conversations and whatever else fits your interests and goals. I want you to hear how people actually speak, not just perfectly scripted dialogues recorded at half speed.

Our sessions are also a way to discover the country behind the language — its people, humour, habits, culture and everyday life. I choose content carefully according to your needs and interests, but the long-term goal is for you to start exploring on your own and find things you genuinely want to watch, read and listen to.
