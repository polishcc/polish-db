    Train Your Polish Like a Champ

    My approach to language coaching is like training an athlete: we define a clear goal, break it into measurable milestones, practise consistently, analyse performance and adjust the training as you progress.

    We do not waste valuable class time on things you can do effectively by yourself; instead, I give you relevant and interesting material to work with EVERY DAY over the week for just 10-15 minutes every day.

    Each day, you send me a short text or voice message telling me what you read, watched or listened to, what you understood in Polish, what interested you and what you found difficult. I collect this language production over time, which allows me to see what you already have a strong grasp of, where patterns of difficulty are appearing and what we should work on next.

    This also allows us to use spaced repetition—returning to important vocabulary and structures at carefully timed intervals—and retrieval practice, where you actively recall and use language rather than simply rereading or reviewing it. We also use deliberate practice: focused work on the specific skills that currently limit your performance, with immediate feedback and repeated opportunities to improve them.

    Our classes therefore follow a clear flow: we review your engagement with Polish during the week, discuss what caught your interest, what you understood, what remained unclear and, most importantly, what you have begun to make your own. We then work directly on the areas you find most difficult—which I will often already have identified from your daily messages—before introducing new concepts, situations, dialogues, vocabulary and structures that build naturally on what you already know. You will be able to practice these area during your every day learning sessions during the week on your own while sending me your results.

    Class time is particularly devoted to activities that benefit from a coach being present: conversation, pronunciation, correction, experimentation with new language and the kind of immediate feedback that is difficult to obtain when studying alone.

    Throughout the process, I monitor engagement and progress, adjust the programme, set clear milestones and celebrate achievements, so that every week of practice moves you closer to the level of Polish you want to perform at.
